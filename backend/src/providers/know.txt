7. providers/ — External APIs

This folder is particularly important for your project.

Your document already specifies a provider layer for Google Maps, RailRadar, Amadeus, Uber and Open-Meteo.

I would structure it like:

providers/
├── googlemaps/
│   ├── places.js
│   ├── routes.js
│   └── index.js
│
├── railradar/
│   ├── trains.js
│   ├── stations.js
│   └── index.js
│
├── amadeus/
│   ├── flights.js
│   ├── hotels.js
│   └── index.js
│
├── uber/
│   └── index.js
│
└── openmeteo/
    ├── weather.js
    └── index.js

But based on the review we discussed, I would now remove uber/ from the MVP.

Use:

providers/
├── googlemaps/
├── railradar/
├── amadeus/
└── openmeteo/
